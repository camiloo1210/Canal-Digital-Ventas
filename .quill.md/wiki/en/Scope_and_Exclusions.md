# Project Scope and Exclusions

## Scope of the selected solution
The project starts with the requirements gathering and architecture design phase, and covers up to the development, testing, and production deployment of the responsive web application. Within this boundary, the system exclusively features:
- Automated notifications via WhatsApp.
- Exploration and search in the digital catalog.
- Management of commercial orders and payment processing.
- Metrics visualization in an analytical dashboard.
- Profile and permissions control.
- General administration of products and wholesale prices.

All divided into six main modules. The project also defines certain activities that are outside the scope.

## Project Exclusions
The project does not include the following functionalities:
- **National shipping logistics**: The system will manage internal order statuses (e.g. Confirmed, Shipped, Delivered), but will not feature integrations with third-party platforms (couriers like Servientrega or LaarCourier) for satellite tracking or dynamic freight quotes.
- **Automated electronic billing (SRI)**: The system will not automatically issue electronic invoices. The Stationery Store will operate under its RIMPE Popular Businesses regime, issuing physical sales notes or managing invoices outside the system upon buyer request.
- **Native mobile application**: Downloadable apps for Android (APK) or iOS (IPA) will not be developed. The solution will be strictly a responsive web application.
- **Supplier purchases and automated physical inventory module**: Backward supply chain (stationery store suppliers), accounts payable, internal expense management, and integration with barcode scanning hardware at the physical counter will not be managed.
- **Hardware maintenance**: The project excludes any type of technical support for the stationery store's physical computer equipment.
