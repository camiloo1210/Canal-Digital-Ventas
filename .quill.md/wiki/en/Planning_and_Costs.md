# Planning and Operational Costs

## Product Backlog and Sprints Planning
Technical execution will be structured through a planning of 13 iterations (Sprints) of 1 week each, aligning with the 4-month academic semester and adapting to the capacity of a team made up of 2 developers.
The first 10 Sprints will address the specific functional groups, while Sprints 11, 12, and 13 will be dedicated exclusively to comprehensive testing, QA, and production deployment.

## Development and Operation Budget (4 Months)
The calculation includes the Hexagonal Architecture, the dimensioned team, and the cloud infrastructure necessary to guarantee performance, security, and asynchronous processing.

### Development Costs (Labor)
- Cost per developer (Monthly): $720.00 USD
- Total team cost (Monthly): $1,440.00 USD
- **Total Labor (4 months)**: $5,760.00 USD

### Infrastructure and Equipment Costs (Fixed Capital)
- Computing Equipment: 2 computers × $950.00 = $1,900.00 USD

### Monthly Operating Budget (SaaS and Licenses)
The operational cost of the project using basic professional plans (excluding Free Tiers):
- **IDE Licenses / Tools**: $38.00 USD (Code assistance and structured development)
- **GitHub Team**: $8.00 USD (Branch rules and CI/CD)
- **Figma Professional**: $30.00 USD (Version history and design libraries)
- **Vercel Pro**: $40.00 USD (Commercial deployments without interruptions)
- **Supabase Pro**: $25.00 USD (Backend as a Service, backups, and 8GB storage)
- **Total Monthly Operating**: $141.00 USD
- **Total Operating (4 months)**: $564.00 USD

**Overall Project Total**: $7,660.00 USD

## Benefits of SCRUM Execution
1. **Risk and Integrations Management**: Iterative deliveries facilitate early validation of critical integrations with third-party services (Lemon Squeezy and Meta webhooks).
2. **Technical Adaptability**: Sprints allow managing uncertainty and adapting the frontend and backend components modularly.
3. **Usability Prioritization**: Constant feedback ensures fluid interfaces for the buyer, optimizing conversion rates.
